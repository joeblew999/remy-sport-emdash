# 2026-10-06 — What is left on the Remy-Sport site

**Status:** active — **0 of 5 done** (item 3 is deployed, with one line unproven). The only open plan here. The two plans that got the site this
far are in [`done/`](done/) as the record of why it is built the way it is; their ticks were never
kept up, so read this file, not them, for what is left.

## Where the site stands

Built and reviewed, running locally, and deployed since 2026-10-07 at
`https://remy-sport-site.gedw99.workers.dev`: pages and a blog from the CMS, a page per
event and per organisation read from the app, an edge cache, a sitemap, structured data. A hostile
review found no page that names a person. The app's entries are test data, so those pages carry a
"sample data" notice and stay out of search engines until `APP_DATA_IS_REAL` is switched on.

       and `posts`; `whoami --live` says admin
    5. the content, EmDash's way: `mise run emdash -- site export --output backups/local-….emdash`
